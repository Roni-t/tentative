1. ABOUT THE SERVICE

Tentative helps groups find a time to meet. A host creates an event proposal with candidate dates and a time window and shares it with invitees. Invitees mark their availability, and the host reviews the combined availability heatmap and finalizes a time. Tentative can send invitations, reminders, and updates by text message, and can connect to external calendars if you choose.
